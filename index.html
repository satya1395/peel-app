Common questions

Where are my notes stored? Only on your phone. Peel has no account and sends nothing anywhere.

How do I delete a note? On the Wall, long-press a note, select any others, then tap Delete.

Is there a character limit? Yes, 140 characters, so each note stays short and kind.
